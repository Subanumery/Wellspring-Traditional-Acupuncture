Due to the current Covid-19 situation I am vigilant with infection control in my clinic.

I am not asking you to do anything that I do not expect of myself. By wearing a mask I choose to protect myself, my family, my friends, the community and, in this practice, my patients, particularly those who are more vulnerable.

With this in mind, I ask that you attend your appointment on time so that I am able to welcome you and keep everyone socially distanced and the clinic space disinfected between patients.

I will be wearing an FFP 2 or 3 face mask and will sanitise my hands on your arrival (and throughout your treatment).

On arrival I ask that everyone who comes into the clinic wears a 3 ply face mask or FFP2 mask, which covers your nose and mouth at all times before entering the treatment room. If you don't have one, I am, of course, happy to provide you with one on arrival. I will also ask you to sanitise your hands with alcohol gel, which I will, of course, provide.

Please do not come to the clinic if you have signs of Covid-19, have tested positive or have been in recent contact with someone who has tested positive. If you have had Covid-19 please do not attend the clinic until you have been symptom free for 10 days.

All these precautions are to keep everyone safe.

Thank you.
