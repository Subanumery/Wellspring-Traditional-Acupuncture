Who Has Acupuncture?

The value of acupuncture has long been known for the treatment of pain. However, acupuncture can treat a variety of physical, emotional and mental health problems.

As acupuncture works with the whole person 'holistically' - mind, body and spirit - benefits often include more than just relief from the main presenting symptoms, with improvements in:

  • Energy Levels
  • Sense of Wellbeing
  • Quality of Life
  • Emotional Health

For these reasons, acupuncture is often used as a preventative treatment aimed at strengthening the constitution to maintain health on all levels or to work with individuals who feel unwell in themselves without being ‘ill’ in a western-medical sense.
